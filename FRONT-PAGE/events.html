Mini-course on credit risk

Mini-course on credit risk, by Viral Acharya, Stern School of Business, New York University at IGIDR, with the following course structure:

  1. Prerequisites: option pricing
  2. Introduction
  3. Structural Models - I
  4. Structural Models - II
  5. Liquidity risk of corporate bonds
  6. Reduced form models: Preliminaries
  7. Single name credit derivatives
  8. Litterman and Iben model
  9. Other reduced form models
  10. Correlation products
  11. The copula approach to valuing correlation products
  12. Correlation modelling
  13. The Merton approach to correlation products
  14. Control variate technique for structured credit index products

All the files in one .zip file.
