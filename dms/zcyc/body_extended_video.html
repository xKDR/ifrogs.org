Daily ZCYC

This page shows daily movement of the ZCYC for the period Jun 2001 - September 2020. The ZCYCs which have 10 year maturity rate greater than 91 day maturity rate are shown in blue color and the ones with 10 year maturity rate less than 91 day maturity rate are represented by red color.
