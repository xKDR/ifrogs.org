Financing patterns of three cohorts of Indian firms

This page presents time series graphs of evolution of the capital structure and financing preferences of three cohorts of Indian non-financial firms, starting from 1993. A cohort is defined in terms of the year of incorporation of the firm, and our chosen three cohorts are Post liberalisation, firms incorporated in 1992/1993/1994, Credit boomers, firms incorporated in 2000/2001/2002,and Post 2008, firms incorporated in 2008/2009/2010.

(Content: Rajeswari Sengupta and Susan Thomas. Implementation: Arpita Pattanaik. Updates: Ami Dagli)
