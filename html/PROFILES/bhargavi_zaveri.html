Bhargavi Zaveri

            Bhargavi Zaveri is a researcher at the Finance Research Group, Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research (IGIDR). At IGIDR, she is part of a team of lawyers and economists who work on the intersection of law, economics and policy, with a focus on Indian financial markets. Previously, she has held research positions at the National Institute of Public Finance and Policy, New Delhi and the Harvard Law School. Some of her work can be accessed here and here. A lawyer by training, Bhargavi has previously worked at a Mumbai-based law firm for six years, where she worked on financial regulation.
