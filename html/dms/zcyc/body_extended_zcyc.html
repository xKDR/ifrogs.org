ZCYC as on last trading day of October

This page presents one ZCYC per year to show how interest rates in the economy have changed over time. The date chosen is the last trading day in October. For example - for the year 2011, the curve represents ZCYC on 31st October 2011 and for the year 2015, the curve represents ZCYC on 30th October 2015.



Instructions for using the plot -

  • Hover over a year legend to highlight that year's ZCYC.
  • Click on a particular year legend to deselect/select it from the plot. For example - clicking on 2011 will remove the ZCYC for 2011 from the plot.
