
At the IGIDR Finance Research Group, we focus on three areas of finance research in India:

1. Financial markets. This includes issues like:
   * Measurement of liquidity and liquidity risk
   * Consequences of liquidity: how does it affect market efficiency
   * Design of market microstructure to obtain liquidity and efficiency
   * Impact of technology on market quality
   * Systemic crises caused by liquidity shocks. Emphasis on vulnerabilities in the developing economies compared to developed markets.

2. Financing constraints: small differences in resource allocation can result in first order impact, particularly in developing economies. Issues targetted here include:
   * How do financially constraints firms behave compared to what is known in the literature?
   * Similarly for financially constrained households.
   * What causes financial exclusion? What changes in information systems facilitate financial inclusion?  What is the role of incentives towards this?
   * How can policy focus on measuring the levels of financial inclusion, rather than imposing constraints on financial innovation that facilitate financial inclusion?
   * How do local innovations such as joint liability groups and chit funds facilitate financial inclusion? How should regulation be structure to facilitate growth of these while managing systemic risk?

3. Financial firms and intermediaries:
   * How does regulation distort the behaviour of financial intermediaries? 
   * How does the distorted behaviour affect resource allocation?
   * What are instances of market failure in developing economies, and are they different from those in more developed markets?
   * What are effective government interventions to address market failures?
