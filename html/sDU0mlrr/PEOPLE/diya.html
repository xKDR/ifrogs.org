Researcher

Diya Uday
Senior Research Consultant (Legal)





Diya Uday is a lawyer and research consultant at the Finance Research Group. Her areas of work include land, agriculture and commodity markets. She has worked on projects with the Ministry of Finance, the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) and the Omidyar Network. Prior to joining IGIDR, she has been an associate at law firms including Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas in India where she has worked in the areas of land and property law, project finance and structured debt.
