Call for Papers: IGIDR Field Workshops, 2017

The Finance Research Group at Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research is inviting submission of papers for Field Workshops during 2017. Each field workshop will discuss six papers in a day, dedicating a full hour to each paper for presentation, discussions by an expert in the field and the general audience. Details are as follows:

  • Household Finance Research, 1st April 2017

    Invited talk: Tarun Ramadorai, Imperial College London

    Submission deadline: 5th March. Acceptance: 8th March

    Details at http://www.ifrogs.org/EVENTS/hhfin2017.html

  • Financial Markets Research, 6th May

    Submission deadline: 26th March, Acceptance: 2nd April

    Details at http://www.ifrogs.org/EVENTS/finmkts2017.html

  • Firm Finance Research, 24th June, 2017

    Submission deadline: 20th May. Acceptance: 27th May.

    Details at http://www.ifrogs.org/EVENTS/firmfin2017.html

  • Mathematics and Economics, 10th and 11th August, 2017.

    Details at http://www.ifrogs.org/EVENTS/mande2017.html

Support: IGIDR will provide accomodation at the workshop venue for 2 nights around the dates of the workshop.

Contact details: Submissions must be sent as PDF files, to Jyoti Manke at jyoti@igidr.ac.in, +91-22-28416592 (office) or +91-98205-20180 (cellphone).
