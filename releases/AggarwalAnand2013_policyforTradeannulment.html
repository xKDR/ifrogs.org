Response to SEBI's Discussion Paper: Review of policy for trade cancellation/annulment

Nidhi Aggarwal, Chirag Anand


This policy note is a response to the SEBI discussion paper on trade annulment in securities markets. The response note discusses various issues concerning trade cancellation and its impact on the markets. It further suggests possible alternative solutions.

Citation: Response to SEBI's Discussion Paper: Review of policy for trade cancellation/annulment, Nidhi Aggarwal, Chirag Anand. FRG Policy Note, 2013-11-08 November 2013.
